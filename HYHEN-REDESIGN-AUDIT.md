## Audit of previous live KDYM website

1. **Stale primary call to action:** Home promoted June 1–4 camp after that date had passed. The new hero drives users to the November Hyphen registration.
2. **Outdated hero copy:** "Outpour" and camp-centric information displaced the district's next event. Replaced by focused Hyphen campaign and refreshed KDYM identity.
3. **Information architecture:** Old public Play landing duplicated the app experience; new /play route redirects to /app, retaining old links without maintaining duplicate content.
4. **Redundant/outdated email capture:** /android still asked users for an email despite the app being live. Now redirects to /app, where both verified native store links are available.
5. **Brand continuity:** Kept official white KDYM logo and social destinations; rebuilt homepage and merch with one typography, color and spacing system.
6. **Navigation:** Fixed navigation and one clear retreat registration button, with accessible mobile navigation, anchor links, and visible district context.
7. **Responsive experience:** Mobile breakpoints for hero, product grid, menu and section layouts. Motion is minimized for reduced-motion preferences.
8. **Search metadata:** Added site title, description, OpenGraph, Twitter metadata, sitemap, robots rules and page-specific merchandise metadata.
9. **Link trust:** Registration goes to the supplied ChurchTrac event; directions use the verified host address. App download buttons point to verified Apple and Google Play listings.
10. **Performance:** Merch has an existing ~29.7 MB MP4 and several multi-megabyte PNGs. This remains a material bandwidth and mobile performance concern. The new merch page uses lazy-loaded collection images, but the hero autoplay video should be separately compressed and/or hosted on a streaming CDN. Existing favicon is ~2.2 MB and could also be optimized.
11. **Accessibility:** Semantic landmarks, labels, keyboard-accessible navigation, manual video pause and reduced-motion behavior; verify with real-device and screen-reader testing before production release.
12. **Operational risk:** Firebase-backed native app is intentionally not modified here. No speculative event schedule, dorm information, registration pricing, or eligibility has been invented.

## Visual concept

**Ink / Lilac / Signal:** near-black #080a12, slate surfaces #10131d, lavender #b7a5ff, citrus signal #e4fc8d, chalk #ecece5. Space Grotesk for high-impact typography; DM Sans for longer text.

The visual is original CSS-driven lettering, orbital geometry and scroll motion, not event imagery. No stock photography needed.
